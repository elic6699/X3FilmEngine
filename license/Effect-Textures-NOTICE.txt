FrameMatter effect textures
==========================

The release includes 41 light-leak textures and five whole-frame aging
textures selected and supplied by the FrameMatter maintainer.

Source material: Chemlooks film overlays and Onetake light-leak/aging
textures. Original copyrights remain with their respective creators.
FrameMatter's GPL notice does not claim ownership of these source images
or relicense them. Renaming and SPKL conversion do not change attribution.

The bundled files use neutral FrameMatter filenames. Their manifests
record dimensions and SHA-256; the maintainer retains the original source
mapping and preparation records separately.
